excerpt: The first three days after a peel determine how smooth and even your final result looks. Keep things simple and protective.
---
Use a mild cleanser, fragrance-free moisturizer, and broad-spectrum SPF every morning. Reapply SPF when outdoors.

Avoid scrubs, exfoliating acids, and heat-heavy activities like saunas or steam rooms while skin is settling.

Let flaking skin shed naturally. Picking can trigger irritation and uneven pigmentation, especially in sensitive areas.
