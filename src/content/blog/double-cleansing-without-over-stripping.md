title: How to Double Cleanse Without Over-Stripping Your Skin
category: Routine
date: July 2026
publishedAt: 2026-07-12
readMinutes: 4
excerpt: Double cleansing can improve texture and clarity, but only when the first and second cleanse are chosen for your skin type and done with a gentle touch.
---
Start with an oil-based cleanser to dissolve sunscreen, makeup, and excess sebum. Massage for about 45 to 60 seconds, especially around the hairline and nose.

Follow with a low-foam, pH-balanced cleanser. You are not trying to leave your skin squeaky clean. That feeling usually means the barrier is being compromised.

If your skin feels tight afterwards, reduce cleanse time or switch to a creamier second cleanser. Consistency beats harshness every time.
