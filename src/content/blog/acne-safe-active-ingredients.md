Salicylic acid helps clear pores by dissolving oil and dead skin buildup. Start with two to three nights weekly and monitor for dryness.

Azelaic acid supports clearer skin tone while calming visible redness. It is a versatile option for both acne and post-blemish marks.

Retinoids support cell turnover and long-term texture improvement. Introduce slowly, moisturize well, and avoid stacking too many new actives at once.
